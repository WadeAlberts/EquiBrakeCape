Phase 5 — How PayFast Works in This Project
The checkout flow works like this:

Customer fills in the checkout form and clicks "Pay with PayFast"
Flask saves the order to the database (payment_status = pending_payment)
payfast_helper.py builds a signed form with all order details
The browser submits that hidden form to sandbox.payfast.co.za
Customer pays on PayFast's hosted page
PayFast secretly POSTs an ITN to /payfast/notify on our server
We verify the ITN using the raw POST body (not the decoded dict — this was the critical fix that made signatures match)
Order is updated to payment_status = paid and confirmation email is sent
PayFast redirects customer to /order/confirmation/<order_number>

The confirmation page shows 4 different states based on payment_status in the database: paid ✅ / processing ⏳ / cancelled ❌ / failed ⚠️
